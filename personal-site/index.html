AI, data systems, and academic curiosity

Sotirios Antonios Antonopoulos

I work across AI systems, data engineering, and applied research. I am interested in how models learn, how internal representations evolve, and how to build systems that are both useful and explainable.

Who I am

I am an engineer working at the intersection of machine learning, data platforms, and software systems. My work moves between production settings and research-driven questions: evaluation, interpretability, retrieval, multilingual NLP, and model behavior under real constraints.

Professionally, I care about systems that can be operated, audited, and improved over time. Academically, I am drawn to questions around representation learning, reasoning, information flow, and the structure of internal computation.

What I am interested in

  • Representation learning and internal model dynamics
  • Interpretability beyond surface metrics
  • Reasoning systems and evaluation methodology
  • Reliable AI for multilingual and legal settings
  • Data and AI systems that hold up outside demos

How I spend my academic time

I spend a good part of my time reading and testing ideas rather than only consuming model benchmarks at face value. Lately this has meant working with hierarchical reasoning models, information-based analysis, checkpoint comparisons, and negative-result ablations to better understand what networks are actually doing internally.

I am less interested in presenting systems as black-box successes and more interested in whether we can trace learning, compression, stability, and failure in a disciplined way.

Outside work

I am also a passionate cyclist. I value the same things there that I value in technical work: endurance, rhythm, patience, and paying attention to small improvements over time.

Current base

Based in Luxembourg and currently working on AI and data problems that span legal NLP, validation, RAG systems, and model analysis.
